The first release of Issa Reader for Android: read, listen and read along with your own Storyteller library, on your phone and in Android Auto. It matches Issa Reader 1.5.2 on iPhone: Find and Marks in the reader's top bar, Stop Narration, the book's title under the reader's controls, and Ask about this book on phones with Gemini Nano, off until you turn it on. Runs on Android 11 and later.
